# Toptalla: digital presence, marketing and ASO

Routes: [Toptalla hub](index.md) · [competitor analysis](../index.md) · previous: [06 Competitors and positioning](06-competitors-and-positioning.md) · next: [08 Pricing and contracting](08-pricing-and-contracting.md)

**Scope of this file:** which channels Toptalla uses, how each could be used and measured, and an acquisition and attribution plan for both sides of the business (B2B salons, B2C customers). App-store listing facts are in [03](03-platforms-and-availability.md); the metrics that close the loop are in [12](12-validation-plan-and-metrics.md).

## Digital presence, marketing and ASO study

| Channel | What appeared in the review | Suggested use and how to measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | [@toptalla.partners](https://www.instagram.com/toptalla.partners/) targets Saudi salon businesses, and about 1,725 followers appeared in the public snapshot. | Short operations videos and customer cases, with a per-campaign link. Measure viewing of the offer that led to a booking and then a paid branch, not likes. |
| **LinkedIn** | The [company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/toptalla) is the B2B positioning reference, and about 598 followers appeared there. | Direct content to chains and managers: multi-branch settlement, permissions, and a return-on-investment assessment backed by data. |
| **YouTube** | The [Toptalla channel](https://www.youtube.com/@toptalla) shows an [introductory video from 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DHFWHTX-sC4). Recent view counts and posting cadence for the channel were not verified. | Separate sales explanation from operations-user training. Tie each video to an onboarding stage and measure the drop in support tickets. |
| **Facebook and X** | A link to [Partners Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/toptalla.partners/) and [X](https://x.com/toptalla_app) appears in signals on the [official site](https://toptalla.com/ar). Independent verification of Facebook activity volume was not possible in the sample. | Supporting channels and retargeting once audience size is proven; the presence of a link does not prove ad spend or ad performance. |
| **TikTok, Snapchat and Telegram** | No reliable official account for them appeared in this study. Toptalla's documentation mentions sharing booking links through TikTok and Snapchat and lists TikTok messages as conditional on account eligibility, which is not proof of an active official account ([booking links](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/features/quick-booking-links), [plan details](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/pricing)). | TikTok and Snapchat are an acquisition test for consumers in a specific area; do not widen spend before measuring cost per completed visit. |
| **WhatsApp and Google Maps** | The company documents receiving and booking from WhatsApp through TopConnect, and recommends putting the booking link in the Google business profile ([TopConnect](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/features/topconnect), [booking from business channels](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/features/online-booking)). | Give each link a clear source, and separate a booking produced by the front-desk employee from self-booking and marketplace booking. |
| **Google Search and the stores** | Indexable city and salon pages and an operations blog exist, and the names and categories of the consumer and partner apps differ in the listings ([Damascus page](https://toptalla.com/ar/places/in-damascus-sy), [business blog](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/blog), [Saudi Apple publisher](https://apps.apple.com/sa/developer/toptalla-information-technology-company/id1626792654)). | Expand city × service pages only if the supply is real and available. In ASO, unify the app names while separating the audiences, test the first three screenshots and the Arabic text, and link web pages to the right store without forcing a download. |

## Acquisition and measurement plan

- **B2B:** local search content + direct outreach to selected branches → an offer built on the salon's data → a trial in which services and calendar are prepared → first completed visit → paying branch → 90-day retention. Do not treat free sign-up as activation.
- **B2C:** a city page, salon link or app → a bookable service → a confirmed appointment → a completed visit → a return within a period suited to the service. Test each city, service and channel separately.
- **Attribution:** keep the first-discovery source and the appointment-creation source, define a new marketplace booking versus known customers, then tie the event to a unified identifier as it moves between web, chat and app. Keep the phone number, chat text and any sensitive detail away from advertising analytics tools.
- **Best SEO opportunity:** fix the consistency of country, address and currency before multiplying pages, and watch indexed pages that have real appointments and non-branded queries. Traffic or keyword rankings are not estimated in this study without measurement.
