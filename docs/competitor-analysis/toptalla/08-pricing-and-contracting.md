# Toptalla: pricing and contracting

Routes: [Toptalla hub](index.md) · [competitor analysis](../index.md) · previous: [07 Digital presence and marketing](07-digital-presence-and-marketing.md) · next: [09 Financial feasibility](09-financial-feasibility.md)

**Scope of this file:** what Toptalla publishes about plans, commission and payment activation, and what a buyer must ask for in writing to get a true total cost. The buyer's and builder's economics built on these prices are in [09](09-financial-feasibility.md); data-processing and contract terms are in [11](11-privacy-compliance-and-risk.md).

## Pricing and contracting

- **Basic:** the announced offer is **SAR 1,500 including tax for a full year** instead of the visible reference price of **SAR 2,500**, until **31 December 2026**. The page mentions a 14-day trial and setup and data migration available at no charge, and notes that new prices start on **1 January 2027** and will be announced later; ask for what your current offer includes compared with the 2027 feature table ([official pricing](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/pricing)).
- **Plus and Pro:** the company page shows, in Plus, a site under the center's name, a messaging account within TopConnect, reports and follow-up of incomplete bookings; and in Pro, two messaging accounts, a WhatsApp agent and advanced tracking features, depending on setup. There is no specific public pricing for these two plans on the page; it distinguishes between the quota of operations-assistant requests and the quota of WhatsApp-agent conversations ([plan comparison](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/pricing)).
- **Commission:** the professional marketplace page describes a new-customer commission according to the plan and the marketplace terms, **0% for returning marketplace customers**, and a separate payment-processing fee. This is more precise for contracting than the general "no commissions" message that appears on LinkedIn; ask for the definition of a new customer, the commission rate, its calculation basis, and what happens on a refund ([published marketplace commission policy](https://business.toptalla.com/en/features/marketplace), [LinkedIn message](https://www.linkedin.com/company/toptalla)).
- **Separating activation from prepayment:** the pricing table mentions activating electronic payment through Toptalla for the site, while the booking feature presents prepayment as a setting that can be turned on. The existence of the integration does not mean every customer will pay a deposit on every booking ([pricing](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/pricing), [online booking](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/features/online-booking)).
- **Total cost:** ask for a line for each of: the subscription, branches and add-ons; Meta messages and overage; AI; domain; device; payment, settlement, refund and dispute fees; marketplace commission; training and export. Compute the 12-month cost and the renewal price, not the cheapest advertised price.
