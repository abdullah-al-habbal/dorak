# Toptalla: platforms and availability

Routes: [Toptalla hub](index.md) · [competitor analysis](../index.md) · previous: [02 Company and evidence](02-company-and-evidence.md) · next: [04 Product and user experience](04-product-and-user-experience.md)

**Scope of this file:** on which surfaces Toptalla exists (web, salon-branded sites, Android, iOS, macOS/Vision, other desktops), what the public store data shows, and where verification stops. Features are in [04](04-product-and-user-experience.md); how the surfaces are used for acquisition is in [07](07-digital-presence-and-marketing.md).

## Platform and availability study

| Platform | Audience and what is established | Verification limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketplace web** | Center discovery, filters, service pages, ratings and offers, and a booking-start path, in Arabic and English ([marketplace in Arabic](https://toptalla.com/ar), [marketplace in English](https://toptalla.com/en)). | Display and results are affected by country and filter; the public page is not a measure of attendance or payment. |
| **Partner web** | The business site links to sign-in and sign-up and shows the operations and management functions; no operations screen inside a signed-in account was examined ([announced business portal](https://business.toptalla.com/ar)). | Do not assume web features match mobile or match across plans. |
| **Salon-named sites** | A published Sand spa example shows packages and services and leads to booking; the custom site and the service link are channels different from the discovery marketplace ([Sand spa site](https://sandspa.my.toptalla.com/ar), [booking links](https://business.toptalla.com/ar/features/quick-booking-links)). | Verify domain ownership, whether it can be moved, and conversion tracking after leaving the hosting. |
| **Android for customers** | Google Play Saudi Arabia shows the **100+ downloads** band and a last update of **30 July 2026**; no overall rating for the app itself appeared on the page read ([Google Play customers, Saudi Arabia](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.toptalla.marketplace&hl=ar&gl=SA)). | The US-English page shows 10+ and an older update date; the two snapshots are not merged and are not suitable for measuring users per country ([Google Play, US interface](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.toptalla.marketplace&hl=en_US)). |
| **Android for partners** | Google Play Saudi Arabia shows **500+ downloads**, **5.0 from 12 reviews**, and an update on **11 November 2026** ([Google Play partners, Saudi Arabia](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.toptalla.business&hl=ar&gl=SA)). | A small number; not enough evidence of general satisfaction, nor of the number of paying salons. |
| **iPhone and iPad for customers** | An official listing named "Toptalla" in the [Saudi App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/toptalla-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A8-%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A9/id6759287373), with a [direct link in the US store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/toptalla-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A8-%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A9/id6759287373), although it did not appear in the snapshot of the [US publisher page](https://apps.apple.com/us/developer/toptalla-information-technology-company/id1626792654). The Saudi listing has no sufficient ratings summary, and the minimum is iOS/iPadOS 15.6 ([Apple Saudi Arabia](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/toptalla-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A8-%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A9/id6759287373)). | That a listing opens does not mean an install, activation or payment was tested with an account from every country. |
| **iPhone and iPad for partners** | The Saudi App Store shows **5.0 from 4 ratings**, version **2.224.3** dated **8 November 2026**, and a minimum of iOS/iPadOS 15.0 ([Apple Saudi Arabia, partners](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/toptalla-business/id6448799066)). | Data may differ between listing pages and countries; the ratings sample is small. |
| **macOS and Vision** | Apple's listings show compatibility with Macs with an M1 chip or later and with Apple Vision, with an explicit warning that the two iPad apps are not verified for macOS ([customer app](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/toptalla-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A8-%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A9/id6759287373), [partner app](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/toptalla-business/id6448799066)). | A compatibility listing does not prove a separate native desktop app or an officially supported experience. |
| **Windows, Linux and other devices** | The web is the first verification path; no documented native desktop app for them appeared on the [business site](https://business.toptalla.com/ar). | Check the browser, printer, scanner and payment terminal specifically, and do not assume support for every cash-register device. |

## What was not verified

The availability of the two apps for installation with Syrian store accounts was not verified, nor was Huawei AppGallery, PWA installability, or offline support. The existence of Syrian salon pages on the web is evidence of a public offer in the marketplace, not evidence that app or payment features are identical between the two countries ([Syria catalog](https://toptalla.com/ar/places?countryCode=SY)). The Syria-versus-Saudi differences are examined in [05 Market and geography](05-market-and-geography.md), and the real-device test plan is in [12 Validation plan and metrics](12-validation-plan-and-metrics.md).
