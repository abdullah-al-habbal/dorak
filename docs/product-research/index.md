# Product research

Routes: [documentation graph](../index.md) · [README](../../README.md) · [backlog](../backlog/index.md) · [competitor analysis](../competitor-analysis/index.md)

This folder holds research about Dorak itself: what the product does, for whom, on which evidence, and how it stands in its market. It is separate from [competitor analysis](../competitor-analysis/index.md), which studies other products.

A product study is independent. It is produced from this repository and public sources before any comparison, and it does not read competitor findings while evidence is being collected. Findings are analytical judgments, not requirements; when one turns into work, record it in the [backlog](../backlog/index.md).
